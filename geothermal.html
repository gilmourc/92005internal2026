Tours

Thermal pools and geysers at Te Puia

Te Puia offers two different experiences for visitors to explore the geothermal area and learn about the Māori culture.

Te Rā (Day Experience): A 90-minute guided geothermal and cultural walk.

You can add a 30-minute Māori cultural performance featuring song, dance, and the haka.

Te Pō (Evening Experience): Includes a guided twilight tour, a cultural performance, and a traditional hāngī buffet dinner.
