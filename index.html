Geothermal Sites

The geothermal sites are located in the Rotorua; home to a variety of geothermal features, including geysers, hot springs, and mud pools.

Flax Weaving

The flax weave bags are made by the local Maori people. They are made from flax leaves that have been woven together to create a bag.

Cultural

The necklaces are made by the local Maori people. They are made from a diverse variety of unique materials, including bone, shell, and wood.
